Understanding the Basics of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence encompasses a wide range of technologies that enable machines to mimic human intelligence. It includes subfields such as machine learning, natural language processing, and computer vision. Before embarking on the journey through the Artificial Intelligence Course in Ahmedabad, it's essential to understand the foundational concepts:


    Machine Learning: This is the study of algorithms that improve automatically through experience.
    Deep Learning: A subset of machine learning that utilizes neural networks with many layers.
    Natural Language Processing (NLP): Enables machines to understand and respond to human language.
    Computer Vision: Enables computers to interpret and process visual information from the world.
    Robotics: The field of designing and creating robots that can perform tasks.
    Expert Systems: Programs that simulate human decision-making abilities.
